Settlement -> process of transferring funds and/or securities related to trades and some trade event.
Payer is debited, payee is credited.

In a settlement, there is the risk that once you have given something to another party, they do not give you what they agreed they would.

Settlement Failure -> when a trade, settlement or movement of funds doesn't happen. Causes:
- incorrect data
- infrastructure problem
- money laundering checks
- human error
- insufficient funds for trade a pending security

Herstatt risk -> when there is delay between giving and receiving, during this period a bank can go bust and you may not receive what you were promised.

## Synchronising Transfers
Delivery vs Payment (DvP) -> exchange funds for security
Payment vs Payment (PvP) -> exchange funds in two different securities
Delivery vs Delivery (DvD) -> exchange securities for securities

All the above are synchonised.

## Non-Synchronised Transfers
Free of Payment (FOP) -> non-synchronised security transfer.
Delivery of collateral -> payments or FOP delivery
# Settlement Cycle
Settlement Cycle -> period between agreement of a trade and transfer of ownership
Transaction date is referred to as 'T'
Number of business days taken for transfer to happen is represented as T+x (where x is number of business days). Same day transfer would be T+0
Allows parties to confirm details of transaction, deal with issues and get securities/funds in the right place during this period.
# Settlement Instruction
Settlement Instruction -> Once a settlement is decided upon, there needs to be an instruction send out to the relevant settlement agent (bank, CSD, custodian) to make relevant transfers of securities and/or funds.
