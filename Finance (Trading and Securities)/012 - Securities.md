Securities -> can be a range of financial instruments sold by various parties to raise money. Securities come with variety of rights which Issuer owes to owner of security.
Issuance -> process creating and selling securities
    - New issue = completely new security created and offered for sale
    - Securities has a unique identifier (ISIN, SEDOL, CUSIP)
    - Some securities may be issued only to certain groups of people or may depend on credit rating

Example of rights:
- Rights to future cash flows
- Rights to vote on specified issues
- Rights to exercise certain options

Two main types of securities = shares/stocks or equity (issued by companies), bonds

Securities can be transferred, with the rights transferring over to the new owner seamlessly

Typical bond:
Issuer pays owner some face value on a specified date
Issuer pays regular amount based on fixed rate of interest - the Coupon
If issuer becomes bankrupt, bonds are repaid before equity
Has maturity date when issuer repay funds
(Not absolute for all bonds, some bonds may not pay coupon, or have maturity dates - perpetual bonds, or maybe no fixed rate, the value can be linked to an asset - asset backed securities, bonds that can convert to an equity - convertible bonds)

Typical Equity:
Owners have right to share of profits (dividend)
No maturity date on equities
Owners of share can vote on certain things like members of board of acquisitions
(Some equities may be more or less voting rights or significance compared to other equities)

## Share Registries and Transfer Agents
Securities were originally paper documents and proved ownership (called bearer instruments). They are used to exercise rights and attain payment of funds.
These have been removed to reduce money laundering, tax evasion and theft.
Recording ownership is critical. Now it is stored digitally with an organisation that handles the database (share registry, transfer agent)

Central Securities Repositories (CSDs) -> maintains records of ownership of securities.
Financial services firms outsource management of securities to custodians.
